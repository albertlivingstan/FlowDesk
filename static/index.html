Research integrity notes

These aggregates do not establish NLP model accuracy, cost savings, or bot uptime. For a research paper, collect separately labeled ground-truth emails, compare predictions to expert labels and record actual automation run times.

Suggested metrics: macro F1, weighted F1, confusion matrix, ticket routing precision, median processing latency, duplicate rate, human-review rate and time saved vs manual baseline.
